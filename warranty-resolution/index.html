    Warranty Resolution at Cobalt Ridge Automation¶

    Here is our plan for this exercise:

    1. Create a Cartographer project, give it the business brief, and check what it already knows.
    2. Feed it the evidence a real team would have and review the current state (AS-IS) it builds.
    3. Design the future state (TO-BE), make a live edit, and verify it landed everywhere.
    4. Generate the PDD and trace your edits inside it.
    5. Generate the SDD, bring in Maestro Flow for the stages that suit it, and publish both documents to Automation Hub.

    Goal¶

    By the end of this exercise you'll have taken one process from a blank project to a published Process Definition Document (PDD). You'll have a current-state wiki with provenance on every fact, a future-state design where every change is tied to a specific KPI, and a PDD that carries your own edits. From that PDD you'll generate a Solution Design Document (SDD) and publish both to Automation Hub.

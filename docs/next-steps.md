# Next Steps

!!! tip "You've reached the end of the workshop"

    You've taken a real process from scattered evidence to a published PDD, and followed that design all the way to production.

## Keep building

- **Map a process of your own.** Bring the SOPs, recordings and workshop notes you already have, and let Cartographer ask only what's missing.
- **Start from Process Atlas.** For banking, insurance, healthcare, manufacturing, telecom and utilities processes, adapt an expert-attested Map of Work instead of starting from a blank page.
- **Change the map and regenerate.** Edit a business rule or a stage in your Map of Work, then regenerate the PDD and SDD and see what changes downstream.

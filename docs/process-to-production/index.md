# From Process to Production

!!! tip "Here is our plan for this exercise:"

    1. See how the PDD and SDD you just published travel on, from design to build, run and improvement.
    2. Review the **Solution Design Document (SDD)** you generated in **Cartographer** and sign it off.
    3. Hand the SDD to a coding agent that builds, tests and publishes the solution on the UiPath Platform.

## Goal

By the end of this exercise you'll have followed one design all the way down the line. The Cobalt Ridge SDD you generated in the first exercise becomes a working solution in **Maestro** and **Orchestrator**, and the process map, the documents and the solution end up linked in one place. You'll also see how a change to the map finds its way back to the people, and the agents, who maintain the automation.
